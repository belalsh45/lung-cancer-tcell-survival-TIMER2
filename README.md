# T-Cell Infiltration and Survival in LUAD and LUSC

**Exploratory TIMER2.0 analysis | TCGA lung cancer cohorts**

## Objective
Explore whether estimated tumor-infiltrating CD8+ T-cell and regulatory T-cell (Treg) abundances are associated with survival outcomes in lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD) and lung squamous cell carcinoma (LUSC).

## Data and analysis
- **Tool:** [TIMER2.0](https://compbio.cn/timer2/) (online analysis and visualization; the figures were exported from the web tool, not generated by custom code).
- **Cancer cohorts:** TCGA-LUAD and TCGA-LUSC.
- **Immune abundance estimation:** CIBERSORT-ABS.
- **Immune populations:** CD8+ T cells and regulatory T cells (Tregs).
- **Group comparison:** upper versus lower quartiles (25% tails), as recorded in the analysis notes.
- **Tumor-purity adjustment:** recorded locally as `adj_purity`; the original configuration/export should be checked before specifying exactly how purity was included in the survival model.
- **Outcome:** survival as displayed in TIMER2.0 Kaplan–Meier plots. Original exported figures report HR and p-value; the exact model covariates, group sample sizes and type of p-value should be verified in the platform settings before further statistical interpretation.

## Interpretation
None of the four reported comparisons reached the conventional p < 0.05 threshold. These exploratory results do **not** establish a significant association between the analyzed infiltration categories and survival in either cohort. Visual differences in the curves should not be interpreted as conclusive evidence of prognostic benefit or harm.

## Limitations
- Retrospective analysis of publicly available cohort data; no causal inference.
- Cell infiltration is computationally inferred rather than directly measured by flow cytometry or histology.
- The screenshots alone do not establish exact sample counts, clinical covariates or detailed model configuration.
- No external validation, multiple-testing adjustment or independent Cox-model reanalysis is included here.
